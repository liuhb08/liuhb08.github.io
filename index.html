Rclone Google Drive - Personal OAuth Client

This site exists to serve as the home page for a private, single-user Google OAuth client. That client is used by its owner, on the owner's own computer, to access the owner's own Google Drive through rclone.

  • It has exactly one user: the account owner.
  • It is not distributed, published, or offered to anyone else.
  • It collects no analytics, no tracking, and no personal data.
  • The only Google API scope requested is drive.readonly.
